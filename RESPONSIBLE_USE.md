# Responsible Use Guidelines

These guidelines document the intended use of the TinyStories GPT project. They are not a substitute for a model license, legal advice, a safety evaluation, or deployment controls.

## Intended uses

- Educational study of GPT-style Transformer architecture
- Small language-model experiments
- Tokenization and data-pipeline research
- Demonstrations of next-token prediction
- Non-production story-generation experiments

## Uses that are not supported

Do not use this project for:

- illegal activity;
- fraud, phishing, impersonation, or deceptive conduct;
- malware or cyber abuse;
- harassment, threats, or targeted abuse;
- privacy invasion or attempts to recover private information;
- discriminatory profiling;
- decisions concerning employment, credit, insurance, housing, education, healthcare, policing, or other high-impact domains;
- medical, legal, financial, emergency, or safety-critical advice;
- autonomous systems;
- unsupervised child-facing products;
- presenting generated output as verified fact.

## Known risks

The model can produce:

- hallucinated facts;
- repetitive or contradictory stories;
- biased or inappropriate language;
- malformed text or encoding artifacts;
- text that resembles training examples;
- unpredictable output under adversarial or unusual prompts.

No comprehensive red-team, privacy, memorization, bias, toxicity, or security evaluation has been performed.

## User responsibility

Anyone who deploys, modifies, redistributes, or integrates the model is responsible for:

- performing an appropriate risk assessment;
- reviewing applicable laws and licenses;
- adding filtering, monitoring, access controls, and human review;
- clearly disclosing that outputs are machine-generated;
- preventing reliance on outputs in high-risk contexts;
- testing the modified system rather than relying on this project’s results.

These guidelines express intended responsible behavior. If legally enforceable behavioral restrictions are required for distributed model weights, use an established responsible-AI model license after qualified legal review.
